## About the Series

**Our Beloved Summer** is a South Korean romantic comedy-drama released in 2021. It stars **Choi Woo-shik** as Choi Ung and **Kim Da-mi** as Kook Yeon-soo.

The story follows two former high school lovers who meet again years after their breakup when a documentary they filmed together becomes popular.

### Main Characters

- **Choi Ung** – A quiet and talented illustrator who enjoys living a simple life.
- **Kook Yeon-soo** – A hardworking and independent woman who focuses on her career.

### Why I Like This Series

I like this series because it shows how love, memories, and personal growth affect relationships. It also teaches us that people can change over time, but some memories will always remain special.
